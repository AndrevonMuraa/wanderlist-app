Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: March 6, 2026

WanderMark ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Password (encrypted)
  • Profile name and photo (optional)
  • Bio and location (optional)

Usage Data

We automatically collect:

  • Landmarks you visit and mark as complete
  • Photos you upload with visits
  • Travel diary notes
  • Points, badges, and progress
  • Activity on leaderboards and social features

Device Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash reports and performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the WanderMark service
  • Track your travel progress and badges
  • Display leaderboards and social features
  • Enable friend connections and messaging
  • Send notifications about your activity
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance features
  • Respond to customer support requests

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With other users based on your privacy settings (public, friends, private)
  • On leaderboards (display name and points only)
  • With service providers who assist in operating our app
  • When required by law or to protect our rights
  • In connection with a merger or acquisition

4. Your Privacy Controls

You have control over your information:

Visibility Settings

  • Set a global default privacy level (public, friends-only, or private) that applies to all your content
  • Override visibility for individual visits — each visit can have its own setting
  • Changing your default privacy retroactively updates all existing content that uses the default

Interaction Controls

  • Control who can comment on your content (everyone, friends only, or nobody)
  • Report inappropriate content, comments, or user profiles

Account Management

  • Edit or delete your profile information at any time
  • Deactivate your account (30-day grace period before permanent deletion)
  • Reactivate your account by logging in within the 30-day window
  • Manage friend connections and block users
  • Control notification preferences

5. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. Your data is stored securely on cloud servers located in the European Union (Stockholm, Sweden), with encryption in transit and at rest.

While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Photos & Camera Access

WanderMark requests camera and photo library access to:

  • Take personal photos of yourself at landmarks (primary method)
  • Select existing personal photos from your gallery
  • Add a profile picture

Photo Verification

To earn verified leaderboard points, photos must be personal images where you are clearly visible at the landmark. Photos sourced from the internet or without the user present do not qualify as verified and may result in removal of verified points by our moderation team.

Photos you upload are stored securely and their visibility is controlled by your privacy settings. You can delete uploaded photos at any time. Removing a photo may affect the verified status of the associated visit.

7. Content Reporting & Moderation

WanderMark provides tools to maintain a safe and fair community:

  • You can report visits, comments, photos, or user profiles that violate our guidelines
  • When you submit a report, we collect the report reason and the content identifier to investigate
  • Our moderation team reviews reports and may take actions including removing content or revoking verified status
  • We do not disclose the identity of reporters to the reported user
  • Abuse of the reporting system may result in restrictions on your account

8. Children's Privacy

WanderMark is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Third-Party Services

Our app may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

We may use the following third-party services:

  • Apple Sign-In and email-based authentication
  • RevenueCat for subscription and payment processing
  • Resend for transactional emails
  • MongoDB Atlas for secure data storage (EU servers)
  • Render.com for application hosting

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
